AI4DRR

AI4DRR is a hub to showcase experiments, prototypes, and proofs of concept exploring how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) can support Disaster Risk Reduction (DRR). Our work combines practical experimentation with academic and methodological rigour, applying sound research methods, transparent approaches, and critical evaluation to explore new ways of understanding risk, strengthening preparedness, improving situational awareness, and supporting better decision-making before, during, and after disasters.
